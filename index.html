Welcome to - Frederick Heil's Portfolio

Welcome to my creative corner! I’m currently a passionate graphic design student at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV), where I’m diving deep into the world of visual storytelling, branding, and digital artistry. My journey in the graphic design program is all about exploring the power of visuals to communicate ideas, evoke emotions, and create meaningful connections. Beyond the classroom, I’m putting my skills to work in the dynamic field of garment printing. Working in design for garment printing has been an exciting avenue that allows me to blend my love for art with practical, real-world applications. Each project is a new canvas where I get to experiment with colors, patterns, and styles to bring fresh, unique designs to life on fabric. Whether it’s designing custom t-shirts, promotional apparel, or unique fashion pieces, I strive to create designs that not only look great but also tell a story and resonate with the wearer. My goal is to merge creativity with functionality, ensuring that every piece of garment is a work of art that stands out. Thank you for visiting, and I look forward to sharing my designs, inspirations, and journey with you!
